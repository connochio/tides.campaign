## Stormwreck Isle

A small, mountainous island located in the sea of swords, off of the sword coast.  

It hosts abundant endemic life and vegetation across its scarred and craggy landscape, with an eery magical aura that permeates the land and seas surrounding it.

Constantly besieged by tumultuous and sporadic periods of harsh weather, only the hardiest and the most stubborn of creatures can claim to call this isle 'Home'.

Rumors tell tales of ancient battles fought here between dragons from aeons past, but more recently the small isle has been the subject of heightened scrutiny and intrigue by kingdoms on the mainland.
