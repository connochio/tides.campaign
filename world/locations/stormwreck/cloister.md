# The Cloister

Known as "Dragon's Rest" by the inhabitants, the cloister is a small community nestled into the side of the cliffs on the north-western edge of the isle.

Accessible only from overgrown island paths or by a narrow mountain path from the beach, visitors can't help but look up in awe as a large open-air temple fades into view, perched at the peak of the cliffs and obscured by thick fog the only eases on the approach.

## Lower Level

As visitors approach the cloister from the mountain path, small stone columns denote the boundaries where the inclement weather appears to ease up, and the residents are sure to be waiting with both excitement and curiosity.

The path from the boundary of the cloister continues on an easy incline upwards, following the undulations and edges of the mountain and bordered by a simple wooden fence. It is much better maintained than the path up the mountain, with the inhabitants frequently being seen sweeping and tidying away any dust and detritus that has collected.

Overlooking the sheer drops of the cliffs down to the ocean are 6 small dwellings, expertly carved into the rock and filled with simple furniture.

Further up the path are small sets of stairs where the mountain naturally shifts upwards, and a small wooden shack eerily positioned over the side of the rocks, hosting rudimentary wooden crane with some storage shelves.
