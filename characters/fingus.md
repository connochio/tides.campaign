Fingus is a young Wizard hailing from Waterdeep with a penchant for fire!

FIngus has an obsessive interest in Dragons. Even learning the spell Dragon's Breath to be more like them. He hopes to one day to meet a Dragon face to face and study it, learn all its ways.

He studied and graduated with flying colours from the Blackstaff Academy.
