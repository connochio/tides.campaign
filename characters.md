# The Unknowns

Our titular characters, brought together through happenstance and what some may call a touch of cosmic luck.

Banding together after meeting on the shores of Stormwreck Isle chasing dreams, ambitions and answers to burning questions, the intrepid party set off on an adventure that is sure to go down in either legend or infamy.
